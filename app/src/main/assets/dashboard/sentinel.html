🛡️ Sentinel

Sentinel does not watch a number and hope. It measures the running system, names the root cause, shows the evidence that proves it, repairs what is safe to repair, and then measures again to prove the repair worked. Anything that needs a decision is put in front of you instead of being done behind your back.
Administrator access required. Sentinel reads authentication state, credentials and network internals, so it is not available to a standard web user.
